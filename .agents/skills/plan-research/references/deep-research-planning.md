# Detailed planning for new product research

Load only by trigger from `plan-research/SKILL.md`.

## Sources and quality

For each significant question, specify the type of source, period, sample, relevance, bias, rights of use and method of citation. A competitive interface proves mechanics, but not success or suitability. The secondary source does not replace the primary source if the solution depends on the exact requirement.

## Research with participants

Codex does not recruit or conduct interviews on behalf of the user. Prepare sampling criteria, format, recording method and analysis. Connect each question with a hypothesis and solution; ask about past behavior and specific situations rather than asking to evaluate an abstract idea.

## Analytics

Indicate the event or indicator only if the definition is known. Plan to test data quality, period, audience composition, tracking changes, and alternative explanations. Don't promise causality or statistical significance without a suitable design.

## External systems and data

Record what data will leave the local environment, where it will go, on what basis and what can replace the transfer. Capability-check and actual access are performed after plan approval.

## Synthesis

Describe in advance how findings will be combined around user questions and product solutions, how contradictions are noted, and what kind of confidence is acceptable. For each research line, set the stopping and fallback criteria.

Detailed mode adds only the necessary blocks; Don’t turn your plan into a finished study and don’t collect findings in advance.
