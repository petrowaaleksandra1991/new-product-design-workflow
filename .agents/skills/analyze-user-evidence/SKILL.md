# Analyze user evidence

Work only with materials actually provided or found. The research with participants is conducted by the user; the agent can prepare a protocol, questions, a recording form and then synthesize the responses received.

## Preparing the body

- Assign each material a `source-id`, each significant fragment - an `evidence-id`.
- Include method type, audience, date, context, sample, and limitations.
- Anonymize personal data and do not copy secrets.
- Do not mix direct speech, researcher observation and interpretation.

## Synthesis

1. Highlight the situation, trigger, desired progress, current method, barrier and consequences.
2. Group evidence by similarity of the task, and not by a pre-selected screen.
3. Show persistent themes, contradictions, rare but difficult cases and gaps.
4. Formulate JTBD only when the evidence describes the context, trigger, desired progress, current method, barrier and outcome criterion; otherwise, leave the need or question.
5. Use user stories as a requirement format, not as evidence.
6. For each hypothesis, indicate supporting, contradicting and missing evidence.
7. Do not start a synthesis with a fictitious person or group people only by demographics unless the difference in behavior or situation is confirmed.
8. If there is no direct data from participants, label the job as `provisional JTBD` or `desk-research hypothesis`, and not as an established need.

## User Research Questions

If there is not enough information, suggest a method and neutral questions. For each question please indicate:

- what unknown or hypothesis he is testing;
- why the answer can change the decision;
- what type of response will be observable evidence;
- what suggestive language is prohibited.

Follow the principles of Ask Mom: discuss past actual behavior and specific situations, do not ask for evaluation of an abstract idea, and do not turn a compliment into confirmation of demand.

## Exit

Return evidence map, topics, JTBD/needs, user stories if necessary, barriers, segment differences, hypotheses, confidence and plan for closing gaps. For each JTBD, store supporting/contradicting/missing evidence and the decision it influences. Don't call a sample representative without justification and don't promise causality.
