# Detailed check of the new product brief

Load this file only by trigger from `prepare-brief/SKILL.md`.

## Context and system participants

- Separate the user, buyer, customer, administrator and affected third parties if they are different.
- Check the situation, trigger, environment, frequency, access restrictions and current workaround.
- If different audiences or conditions are possible, show how the value proposition and the main scenario change.

## Outcomes and risk

Link the business problem to observable behavior, user outcome, product outcome, business outcome, and guardrails. Don't make up a baseline, target or uplift. For mandatory initiatives, the outcome could be risk reduction, compliance, or cost of service.

## Limitations

Separately check applicable privacy, safety, accessibility, legal, financial, medical and industry restrictions. Use current primary sources only when the review is truly relevant to the task; Do not turn the brief itself into legal research.

## Production quality

- Don't disguise the solution as a problem.
- Do not combine different problems into one function without explanation.
- Indicate the cost of an erroneous decision and reversibility.
- Mark dependencies on data, operations, content, integrations and owners.
- If the critical context is unknown, do not declare the brief complete without the user choosing to respond, explore multiple scenarios, or continue with an explicit assumption.

The detailed scan expands only the relevant sections. The final brief should still be readable and solution-oriented.
