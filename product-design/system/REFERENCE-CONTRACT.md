# Contract of references and user instructions

For each source, store the `REF-ID`, path or URL, access date, owner, permitted use, purpose and weight.

Assignments: `required-asset`, `logic-reference`, `visual-reference`, `mood-only`, `avoid`. Weight: `must`, `prefer`, `consider`, `avoid`. If the purpose or weight is unclear and changes the result, check before assembly.

The user's choice after benchmarking is recorded as a decision: source, specific aspect, reason, adaptation and associated requirements. Do not copy a competitor’s brand, protected assets or unique design without the right.

The user's indication takes precedence as a product decision, but does not turn a guess into a fact. If there is a conflict with research, availability, ethics, or a technical limitation, show the conflict and ask for confirmation of the compromise.
