# Acceptance plan: New Product Design Workflow

The release candidate is checked against the structure, routing and scripts below. Pass requires zero structural errors; an external MCP is not required to test local logic.

1. Clean copy and initialization without overwriting.
2. Research → independent review with a stop between stages.
3. User-selected competitor pattern maintains provenance and adaptation.
4. Timeline/IA → wireframes → read-only wireframe review.
5. Reference intent distinguishes between asset, logic, visual, mood and avoid.
6. Exploratory screens do not become components automatically.
7. Component nomination and formalization require two separate solutions.
8. Visual QA works without ready-made tokens and fixes egress/write gates.
9. Refero/UX Pilot remains evidence/generation providers: domain gates and independent review are not allowed.
10. Missing Miro does not block architecture or wireframes.
11. Multi-screen flow gets an adaptive overview board, interface-copy review and applicable state stress without a new mandatory stage.
12. Brief and research plan use fast path; deep references are loaded only on a justified trigger.
13. An individual performer receives a review of the exact version; if no fresh context is available, the result is not said to be independent.
14. UX test results submitted by the user provide findings and a list of affected versions, but do not rewrite approved documents or layouts without the user's decision.
15. Patterns → PRD → architecture start with approved results and reveal detailed evidence by ID, maintaining review restrictions.
16. `PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md` changes only after a stable product decision, and `EMERGING-SYSTEM-STATUS.md` - after a decision on the direction or system element; hypothesis and `candidate` do not automatically become standard.
17. The Windows PowerShell validator counts discovery characters from UTF-8 text, and not from erroneously decoded Cyrillic.
