# Acceptance: brief with unknown context

## Goal

Ensure that a new idea does not become one randomly selected product concept unless the context of use is known.

## Login

The idea of the product and the expected benefits are given. The circulation situation, environment and audience restrictions are not defined. Different answers change the basic scenario. Some background information is also missing.

## Expected behavior

1. The agent uses what is already known and highlights only conceptually important gaps.
2. He asks several targeted questions with an explanation of what decision depends on the answer.
3. If there is no answer yet, he offers 2-3 different scenarios as hypotheses: who, when, under what conditions and how the product changes as a result.
4. The agent waits for the user's choice or permission to explore multiple scenarios without declaring the invented context as a fact.
5. The final `BRIEF.md` is short and solution-oriented; minor unknowns are left for the next stage without a long questionnaire.

Pass: All five conditions are observed in a real run of the `prepare-brief` stage.
