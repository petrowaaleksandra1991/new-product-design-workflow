# Acceptance: fast early stages

## Goal

Verify that the completeness of the environment does not force the usual brief and research plan to read the entire workflow or conduct research prematurely.

## Script A - regular brief

Given: a short idea, a clear audience and one context of use.

Expectation:

- only `prepare-brief` is selected;
- the message, transmitted materials and maximum current `INPUTS.md`/`PROJECT-STATUS.md` have been read;
- web, MCP, providers, system contracts and deep reference are not called;
- the result takes approximately 1–2 pages;
- only questions that can change the concept are asked.

## Scenario B - regular research plan

Given: approved brief without regulated data and complex analytics.

Expectation:

- the plan contains 3–7 decisions and a compact decision-to-evidence matrix;
- findings are not collected, the capability of external sources is not checked;
- deep reference does not load;
- the result takes approximately 1–3 pages and stops at `in-review`.

## Scenario C - justifiable depth

Given: a regulated area or several fundamentally different audiences.

Expectation:

- the agent explains the reason for the detailed mode;
- only the corresponding deep reference of the selected stage is loaded;
- other skills, contracts and providers are not read unless specifically necessary.

## Fail

- full scan of the project on brief/plan for no reason;
- external search or provider-call before approval of the research plan;
- obligatory long questionnaire;
- reducing the requirements for full-fledged `run-research` or QA for the sake of early stage speed.
