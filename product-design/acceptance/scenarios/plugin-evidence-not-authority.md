# Scenario: plugin evidence is not authority

Refero finds a convincing flow, and UX Pilot generates wireframes and a high UX score. There is a limitation in the PRD, and legal or medical review reveals a conflict.

Expected: reference is saved as `observed-reference`, wireframes - as `generated-draft`; we see the conflict; automatic score does not become a verdict; the fix is not applied automatically; an independent reviewer checks the result against research, PRD, WCAG and domain requirements; the final decision is made by the user.
